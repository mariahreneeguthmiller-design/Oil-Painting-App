Recipe options

This is a starting estimate, not a guaranteed match. Oil paint mixes subtractively, and real results depend on pigment concentration, opacity, tinting strength, brand, medium, surface, and lighting. The estimate is calculated only from the swatch colors you set in your inventory.

How the estimate works

Each paint's swatch is treated as a reflectance color and mixed with a simplified Kubelka–Munk model, a common approximation of subtractive mixing. The app tries every one-, two-, and three-paint combination from your inventory and suggests the closest, preferring fewer paints when the difference is small.

Pigment codes are shown for reference only. Two paints with the same code can mix differently, so the codes are not used to predict behavior. Percentages are by weight; dense pigments such as cadmiums and earths can make weight and volume differ.

To improve estimates, paint out each tube next to white, adjust its swatch in My paints, and set a relative tinting strength for very strong or weak paints. Always mix a small test, compare it under your studio light, and adjust by eye.

      About Pigment & Light

      A personal studio tool for keeping track of your oil paints, sampling colors from reference images, and getting a measurable starting recipe from the tubes you own.

      What the recipe means

      Recipes are starting estimates. They come from the swatch colors you enter and a simplified model of subtractive mixing. Real oil paint depends on pigment concentration, opacity, tinting strength, brand, medium, surface, and lighting, and a photo or screen adds its own color shifts. Pigment codes are for your reference; they don't predict exactly how a paint mixes. Weigh out a small test batch, compare it with your reference under studio light, adjust by eye, and write down what worked on the mixing card.

      Your data

      Everything is stored in this browser on this device, with no account and no server. Your reference images are never uploaded or saved. Use Download full backup on the My paints or Saved mixes screen to keep a copy, and Restore from backup to load it on another device or browser.

      Add to your iPhone Home Screen

      1. Open this page in Safari.
      2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
      3. Tap Add. Pigment & Light opens full screen, like an app, and works offline.

      The Home Screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari. To move paints you entered in Safari, download a backup in Safari and restore it in the Home Screen app.

      Printing mixing cards

      Use Print mixing card in the mixer or on a saved mix. If background colors don't print, turn on "Background graphics" in your browser's print options. On iPhone you can also choose Save to Files from the print preview's share menu to keep a PDF.
